# T15: Shared interface components, and Mission risks and Mental Health as Preact

Part of the move to Preact components (AGENTS.md section 3a, `docs/decisions.md`). This task sets the pattern the
other conversions copy, so keep it plain and readable.

## Why

Sections build their HTML from JavaScript strings and wire events by hand. The interface is moving to Preact
components mounted as Astro islands. Mission risks and Mental Health are finished and nobody else is editing them,
so they are converted first, together with the small shared components every section needs.

## You own

- `src/components/ui/**` (new)
- `src/components/sections/RiskMap.astro`, `src/components/riskmap/**`, `src/scripts/riskmap.js`,
  `src/styles/riskmap.css`
- `src/components/sections/MentalHealth.astro` (new; ask the coordinator for its line in
  `src/components/Sections.astro`), `src/components/mental/**`, `src/scripts/mental.js`,
  `src/scripts/mental-page.js`, `src/styles/mental.css`, `test/mental.test.js`
- In `src/scripts/sections.js`: only the lines that create and initialise the mental health page

## Do

1. Shared components in `src/components/ui/`, each a small Preact component styled with the `--am-*` tokens in
   `src/components/ui/ui.css`: `Panel` (titled surface), `Button` (primary, soft, danger; real `<button>`),
   `Pill` (toggle with `aria-pressed`), `Field` (label, input, help text, error), `Tabs` (with correct
   `role="tablist"` semantics and arrow-key movement), `LevelBadge` (ok, info, warn, urgent: text plus colour, never
   colour alone), `SourceLink` (external link with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`), `Findings` (renders a
   `[{ level, title, saw, todo }]` list). Look at what Mission risks, Mental Health and Emergencies render today
   and extract what repeats; do not design components nobody uses yet. Document each in a short comment at the top
   of its file: what it is for and its props.
2. Mission risks: `RiskMapView.jsx` with child components (mission picker, risk row, rating chip). All data comes
   from `src/scripts/risks.js`; the mission type is read and written with `useStored("astromed-profile", null)`.
   Delete the string-building code from `src/scripts/riskmap.js`; what remains there is the sidebar registration.
3. Mental Health: `MentalHealthView.jsx` with child components (mood check-in, questionnaire, result, urgent
   response, history). Scoring, bands, the flag rule, history and `analyze` input stay in `src/scripts/mental.js`
   untouched in behaviour, with their tests. `src/scripts/mental-page.js` is deleted once nothing imports it.
4. Behaviour and wording must not change. Before you start, take screenshots of every state of both sections
   (risk map for two missions; mood, both questionnaires, a result, the urgent response, history) into
   `docs/evidence/T15/before-*.png`; afterwards take the same shots as `after-*.png` and compare them yourself.
   Differences other than sub-pixel layout need a reason in your report.
5. The urgent response rule from ticket 12 is safety relevant: any answer other than "Not at all" to the
   suicidal-thoughts item shows the response above the result, whatever the score. Show it in an after screenshot.

- Before and after screenshots are in `docs/evidence/T15/` and match.
