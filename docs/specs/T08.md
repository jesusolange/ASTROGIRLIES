# T08: Today page, work queue, and readiness from the risk map

Tickets 4 and 5 in `docs/tickets.md`.

## Why

Overview and Readiness both lead with the same score and Missions holds a separate daily task list: three pages
answer "what should I do today". The readiness weights are hand-picked, and the page showed 100/100 "Ready" from
three heart-rate readings covering two of seven areas.

## You own

- `src/scripts/today.js` (already imported by `main.js`), `src/styles/today.css`
- `src/scripts/readiness.js`, `src/scripts/missions.js`
- `src/scripts/immune.js`, `src/styles/immune.css` (new)
- In `src/scripts/sections.js`: only the Overview page (`renderDoctor`, the `doctorPage` element, the `.ov-ready`
  handler). Do not touch the navigation block or `analyze()`.
- In `src/pages/index.astro`: only the sidebar buttons for Readiness, Missions and Overview, and which button is
  `active` at start
- `test/readiness.test.js`, `test/today.test.js`

## Do

### Readiness (ticket 4)

- Area weights come from `areaWeights(currentMission().id)` in `src/scripts/risks.js`: one weight per monitored
  area, from NASA's in-mission ratings for the chosen mission. Map each area key (`overview:heart`, `nutrition`,
  `vision`, ...) to the data that scores it. An area whose section does not exist yet (check
  `window.AstroMedNav.has`) or has no data counts as missing, not as healthy.
- Coverage rule: when the areas that have recent data carry less than a stated share of the total weight (choose
  the threshold, keep it in one constant, label it on screen as our design choice), show "Not enough data" with the
  list of what is missing and a button to each missing check, instead of a score and a band. Low coverage must never
  produce a high score.
- Explain the calculation on the page in plain words: the ratings are NASA's (link to the Mission risks section),
  the formula and the bands are ours. Show each area's weight and its NASA risk.
- Keep `window.AstroMedReadiness.compute`, `findings` and `immuneFindings` working for the PDF report and
  `analyze()`.
- Pure functions for the weighting and the coverage rule, with unit tests: the score changes with the mission, and
  low coverage returns "not enough data".

### Immune watch (ticket 4)

- Move the immune watch out of the Readiness page into its own section registered as `immune` ("Immune watch"),
  with Record, History and What to do.
- Replace the checkboxes with pills (toggle buttons with `aria-pressed`).
- Allow saliva and blood test results to be entered (free numeric fields with unit and test name, plus a note).
  Do not invent reference ranges: with no sourced range, show the value and its trend against the astronaut's
  earlier values, and say that no reference range is applied.

### Today page and work queue (ticket 5)

- One page registered as `today` ("Today"), first in the sidebar after Emergencies, and the page shown after the
  cover and profile dialog. Top: the readiness score or "Not enough data". Then the items due today, ordered by the
  mission's risk ratings, each with the reason it is due and a button that opens its check
  (`AstroMedNav.show(section)`, and for Daily Checkup the body part).
- Reuse the Missions tasks and their automatic ticking (`detect` in `missions.js`): finishing a check removes it
  from the queue without a manual tick. Higher-rated risks come up more often: derive each check's interval from
  its area's rating with one small table (for example score 16 and above daily, 9 to 15 every 2 days, below 9
  weekly), kept in one constant and labelled on screen as our design choice.
- Vision and Emergencies may register later than you: use `AstroMedNav.has` and the public objects
  (`window.AstroMedVision.isDue`, `findings`) when they exist.
- Any `urgent` finding from any section appears at the top of Today with a button to Emergencies when that
  section exists.
- Points, streaks and badges stay, shown in a compact strip at the bottom of Today. VO2 and equipment content from
  Missions stays reachable (a "Training" panel on Today or inside Exercise; say which you chose).
- Remove Overview, Readiness and Missions as separate sidebar entries and pages. Nothing may still link to them
  (search for `show("doctor")`, `show("readiness")`, `show("missions")`, `.ov-ready`).
