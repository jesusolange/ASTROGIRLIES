# T07: Vision (SANS) self-check

Ticket 6 in `docs/tickets.md`.

## Why

NASA rates spaceflight-associated neuro-ocular syndrome (SANS) 4x4 in-mission for a Mars planetary mission
(`src/data/hrr-risks.js`, risk 105), and the app has no vision check.

## You own

- `src/scripts/vision.js` (already imported by `main.js`), `src/scripts/vision-tests.js`, `src/styles/vision.css`
- `test/vision.test.js`

## Do

- New section registered with `window.AstroMedNav.register({ id: "vision", label: "Vision", before: "twin",
  render })`.
- Record: an on-screen self-test, one eye at a time (cover the other eye), right eye then left eye:
  1. Grid distortion (Amsler grid): show the grid with a centre dot at a stated viewing distance; the astronaut
     reports whether any lines look wavy, blurred or missing, and can mark the area on the grid.
  2. Near vision: lines of text or optotypes in decreasing size at a stated distance; the astronaut records the
     smallest line read correctly. Sizes must be physically meaningful: add a one-time calibration (match an
     on-screen bar to a known object width, for example a standard card of 85.6 mm) and store the pixels-per-mm.
  3. Symptoms checklist relevant to SANS (new blur at near, a blind spot, headache with vision change).
- History: every saved result per eye with date and mission day (`window.AstroMedProfile.missionDay()`), and the
  change against the astronaut's own first result (baseline) and previous result.
- What to do: a concrete action for each outcome. Compare against the astronaut's own baseline, not a population
  norm. The thresholds for "changed" and the action wording are meant to come from the team's medic: put them in one
  data object at the top of the module, use conservative defaults (any new distortion, or a loss of two lines or
  more against baseline, leads to "repeat tomorrow and tell the crew medical officer"; sudden loss or a new blind
  spot leads to Emergencies), label them on screen as a design choice pending medical review, and list them under
  `SOURCE-NEEDED:`. Cite NASA's SANS risk page (`url` of risk 105 from `risks.js`) for why the check exists.
- State on screen that this is a screening aid, not a diagnosis, and that a phone or laptop screen is not a
  calibrated instrument.
- `findings()` on `window.AstroMedVision` returning `[{ level, title, saw, todo }]` for the latest result (see
  AGENTS.md section 3), plus `lastResult()` and `isDue(days)` so the Today page can queue the check.
- Store through `window.AstroMedStore.store` under `astromed-vision`.
- Do not edit the 3D body or other sections; a later integration task adds the eyes to the 3D body and the work
  queue.

## Done when

- A result can be saved for each eye, compared with earlier results in History, and leads to an action.
- Unit tests cover the comparison against baseline and the mapping from outcome to action.
- Screenshots in `docs/evidence/T07/`: the grid test, the near vision test, a result with its action, the history.
