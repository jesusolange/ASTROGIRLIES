# T03: Mission risk map, mission type in the profile, Environment

Tickets 3, 10 and 11 in `docs/tickets.md`.

## Why

The app treats every body system as equally important on every mission. NASA's Human Research Roadmap rates 22
risks per mission type. The data and the model are already in the repo (`src/data/hrr-risks.js`,
`src/scripts/risks.js`, `src/data/risk-coverage.js`, tests in `test/risks.test.js`); nothing on screen uses them yet.

## You own

- `src/scripts/riskmap.js` (already imported by `main.js`), `src/styles/riskmap.css`
- `src/scripts/profile.js`
- `src/scripts/environment.js`
- `test/riskmap.test.js` if you add pure helpers

`src/scripts/risks.js` and `src/data/**` are the coordinator's: if the model lacks something, ask.

## Do

### Risk map (ticket 3)

- New section "Mission risks", registered with `window.AstroMedNav.register({ id: "riskmap", label: "Mission risks",
  before: "radiation", render })`.
- Shows the chosen mission type (from `currentMission()`) with a control to change it (writes `missionType` into the
  `astromed-profile` record through `AstroMedProfile`/the store and re-renders).
- Lists all 22 risks from `rankedRisks(mission.id)`, highest first. Each row: NASA short title and full title, the
  in-mission rating as "likelihood x consequence" with NASA's cell colour (`ops.colour`: green, yellow, red) and
  NASA's disposition text, the long-term health rating, a link "NASA source" to `url` (opens in a new tab) with the
  "last published" date, and either a button that opens the app section watching it
  (`AstroMedNav.show(section)`; sections `vision`, `emergencies`, `immune` may not exist yet: show the area name
  without a button when `AstroMedNav.show` has no such page) or the label "Not monitored yet".
- A risk without an operations rating for the chosen mission shows "Not rated for this mission" and sorts last.
- A short explanation on the page: the ratings are NASA's (Human System Risk Board, linked), the ordering and the
  mapping to app sections are ours.
- `findings()` is not needed for this section.

### Profile form (ticket 10)

- Add "Mission type" (the 8 entries of `MISSION_TYPES`, default `DEFAULT_MISSION`) and "Destination" (free text or
  a short list: Moon, Mars, Low Earth orbit) to the setup dialog; save them as `missionType` and `destination`.
- Align "Save profile" and "Later" on one baseline.
- Sex: label "Sex used for energy calculation"; options Female, Male, Prefer not to say. "Prefer not to say" must
  make the nutrition energy target use the average of the female and male results; check how
  `src/scripts/nutrition.js` reads the profile and tell the coordinator in your report if it needs a change there
  (you do not own that file).
- Height: lower the minimum to 100 cm.
- Label and help text contrast is the theme task's job; do not restyle colours.

### Environment (ticket 11)

- Add the location "In transit" (deep space cruise). Check `src/scripts/radiation.js` for the Mars cruise dose rate
  and use the same figure and source; do not invent a number.
- Each location shows a link "Mission risks for this location" that opens the risk map.
- Changing the mission type in Environment must be possible (same control as on the risk map, or a link to it).
- Text fix: "the risks I watch for" becomes "the risks the tool watches for"; "your Overview and report" becomes
  "Today and your protocols".
- Keep the "Keep in mind" notes. Do not add ground or analog missions.

## Done when

- Changing the mission type changes the order of the risk map, and every row shows its NASA source link.
- The mission type chosen in the profile dialog is the one the risk map and Environment show after a reload.
- Sensorimotor, Pharm and Dust show "Not monitored yet".
- Screenshots in `docs/evidence/T03/`: risk map for Mars planetary and for Low Earth orbit short, the profile
  dialog, Environment with "In transit".
