# T17: Nutrition and Exercise as Preact components

Part of the move to Preact components (AGENTS.md section 3a, `docs/decisions.md`).

## Why

Nutrition and Exercise build their HTML from JavaScript strings and wire events by hand. Nutrition was also
reported broken on the public site (GitHub issue 5): confirm what is broken before converting.

## You own

- `src/components/sections/Nutrition.astro`, `src/components/sections/Exercise.astro` (new; ask the coordinator for
  their lines in `src/components/Sections.astro`)
- `src/components/nutrition/**`, `src/components/exercise/**`
- `src/scripts/nutrition.js`, `src/scripts/exercise.js`, `src/scripts/exercise-overview.js`
- `test/nutrition.test.js`, `test/exercise.test.js` (new)
- In `src/pages/index.astro`: only the `exercisePage` block (it moves into `Exercise.astro`)

If `src/components/ui/` exists on `name15` when you start (`git merge name15`), use its components; if it does
not, write what you need inside your own folders and say so in your report. Do not edit `src/components/ui/`.

## Do

1. Find out what is broken in Nutrition. Build, open the section with `tools/shot.mjs --serve dist --eval`, add a
   food, scan path, weekly chart; read the console. Report what you found and fix it in the converted version.
2. Split logic from interface first. Energy and nutrient targets, daily totals, weekly aggregation, exercise
   session validation and weekly overviews become pure functions in `src/scripts/nutrition.js` and
   `src/scripts/exercise.js` with `node:test` tests. Do not change any formula or reference value (the nutrition
   targets cite NASA JSC-67378 and OCHMO-TB-013; "Prefer not to say" uses the average of the two formulas).
3. Nutrition: `NutritionView.jsx` with child components (targets, the plate, add food, "My foods", weekly chart).
   The barcode scanner needs the camera and a network lookup: keep it working, and when there is no camera or no
   connection show a clear message with the manual entry path. Fix the camera button padding (ticket 16 lists it;
   it is one line here).
4. Exercise: `ExerciseView.jsx` with child components (the three device cards rendered from an array, the session
   form per device, the weekly overview). The card images stay in `public/assets/`.
5. Keep the public objects other modules call (`window.AstroMedNutrition` with `render` and `findings`, and what
   `missions.js`, `readiness.js`, `progress.js` and `sections.js` read from Exercise). `render` may become a no-op.
   Stored data keeps its keys and shape: existing records must still load.
6. Before and after screenshots of every state of both sections in `docs/evidence/T17/`; compare them yourself.

## Done when

- Neither section contains HTML built in strings, `innerHTML`, `querySelector` or `addEventListener`.
- The reported Nutrition problem is named and fixed, or shown not to reproduce.
- `pnpm test` and `pnpm build` pass; before and after screenshots match apart from the fix.
