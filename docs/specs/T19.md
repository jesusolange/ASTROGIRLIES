## Do

1. **Vision in the Today queue.** Today's work queue lists checks that are due. Add the Vision self-check with an
   interval taken from the risk model (`rankedRisks` from `src/scripts/risks.js`, the SANS risk) the same way the
   other queue items get theirs; if a fixed interval is used, label it on screen as our design choice. Saving a
   Vision check removes it from today's queue. Its button opens Vision with `window.AstroMedNav.show("vision")`
   (or the `src/lib/nav.js` export).
2. **Urgent findings open the right Emergency card.** In Today's urgent findings, each finding whose subject has a
   card in the Emergency guide gets a button "Open the emergency guide" that opens Emergencies on that card. Add
   the smallest public way to open a card by id (ask the coordinator if it needs a line in the Emergencies view; do
   not change any guide text). The mapping from finding to card id is plain data in a module with a test.
3. **Protocols wording (ticket 8).** The astronaut is alone: no "for the ground team", no "share with the ground
   medical team", no "for your doctor". The report is the astronaut's own record: e.g. "Your mission health
   report. Keep it as your record; if Earth contact exists you can also send it." Check every other user-facing
   string in your files for the same issue.
4. **Figures.** One shared `Figure` component (image, caption, credit line "Title, Author, Licence" linking to the
   source page) using `src/data/figures.js`. Show the eye cross-section in Vision before the grid and near-vision
   tests. Do not place figures in sections you do not own; list in your report where the others should go.
5. Every line you add follows `docs/decisions.md` (actions never depend on reaching Earth).
