# T45: A 3D planet on the cover, from NASA imagery

Owner request. The cover shows a hand-drawn SVG for the destination (`public/assets/cover/earth.svg`, `moon.svg`,
`mars.svg`). Replace it with a rotating 3D globe textured with NASA imagery of that body.

## You own

- `src/components/planet/**` (new: the component and its CSS), `src/scripts/planet.js` (new, pure helpers),
  `test/planet.test.js`
- `public/assets/planets/**` (new: the texture images)
- `docs/references.md` (one row per image)
- The single line in `src/components/cover/Cover.jsx` that renders the planet image, and only once T43 is merged
  into `name15` (T43 owns the cover until then; until it merges, leave Cover.jsx alone and report that the swap
  is the last step)

## Do

1. **Textures** (public domain, NASA or NASA/USGS; open each source page and record the URL and the credit line
   it asks for in `docs/references.md`):
   - Earth (low Earth orbit missions): NASA Visible Earth "Blue Marble" colour map.
   - Moon (lunar orbit and lunar surface): NASA Scientific Visualization Studio "CGI Moon Kit" (LRO LROC colour
     map; the LOLA elevation map is optional, as a bump or displacement map).
   - Mars: a NASA/USGS global colour mosaic (Viking MDIM or the USGS Mars global colour map).
   Downscale each to at most 2048 x 1024 JPEG (bump maps 1024 x 512), keep the total under 3 MB, and bundle them
   under `public/assets/planets/`. No CDN and no runtime download: the cover works offline.
2. **Component** `src/components/planet/Planet.jsx`: `<Planet body="earth" | "moon" | "mars" />` renders a
   three.js sphere (the `three` dependency is already installed) with the texture, soft directional light from
   one side so the terminator shows, a slow rotation (one turn in about 90 s), transparent background, sized to
   fill its box. The mapping from mission type to body lives in `src/scripts/planet.js` with a test, matching the
   cover's current `planetFor` (leo -> earth, lunarOrbit and lunarSurface -> moon, mars -> mars).
3. **Robust**: no WebGL, or a texture that fails to load, falls back to the existing SVG for that body. Under
   `prefers-reduced-motion` the globe does not rotate. The renderer is disposed when the cover closes (no leaked
   WebGL context; the 3D astronaut needs its own).
4. **Credit** shown in Sources through `docs/references.md` and `src/data/figures.js`-style data if Sources reads
   it; no credit text on the cover itself.
5. Keep the cover's current size and position for the planet, so the layout does not move.

## Done when

- The cover shows a rotating, lit, NASA-textured globe for Earth, Moon and Mars depending on the destination,
  offline after one load.
- With WebGL disabled the old SVG shows.
- `pnpm test` and `pnpm build` pass. No screenshots committed (you may keep checks in `.tmp/`).
