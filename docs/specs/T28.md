# T28: Shared kit and the Check deck frame

Batch 2. One card, one step list, one tab style, one chip, one dialog, one toast. Batch 3 builds every check from
these, so this ticket is components and a demo, not a screen.

## You own

- `src/components/ui/**`, `src/lib/ui.js`
- `src/components/check/**` (new: the deck)
- `src/data/terms.js` (new), `test/terms.test.js`, `test/deck.test.js` (logic only)

## Do

1. **`CheckCard`**: title, optional illustration slot on the right, body, Save. Save runs the card's validation;
   an error shows under its field (`Field` with `error`) and that field takes focus. After a save the card shows
   the result (`LevelBadge`, one sentence of what was seen, one action) and a "Next" button, then leaves the deck
   with a short slide (150 to 250 ms, none under `prefers-reduced-motion`).
2. **`toast({ text, undo })`** in `src/lib/ui.js`: one toast region, bottom centre, `role="status"`. With `undo`
   it shows an Undo button for 8 seconds.
3. **`Steps`**: one numbered step list for device instructions and emergency stages. Readable in both themes;
   the number is plain text in a bordered circle, no filled background.
4. **`Term`**: a dashed-underline term that opens its definition in a `popover` on click or Enter.
   `src/data/terms.js` holds the definitions: HRV, ALARA, ARED, T2, CEVIS, N-telopeptide, GCS, SANS, ICR (read
   where the app uses it; if you cannot establish its meaning from the source, write `SOURCE-NEEDED: ICR` in your
   report and leave it out), and any other abbreviation you find in user-facing text. Definitions are plain
   expansions, one sentence, no thresholds.
5. **`Chips`** (single and multiple choice), **`Tabs`** (the one tab style; delete the others' CSS in `ui.css`
   only), **`Dialog`** (focus trap, Esc closes, returns focus), **`SourceLink`** (opens the Sources dialog at a
   source id; until T29 lands it links to the source URL).
6. **Icons**: an `Icon` component that maps the app's icon keys to `lucide-preact` icons, `aria-hidden` by
   default. T26 removes the old SVG sprite from the shell; do not touch it.
7. **Deck** (`src/components/check/CheckView.jsx`): collects cards with
   `import.meta.glob("../*/cards.js", { eager: true })`, orders them like Home's due list, shows the due cards as
   a stack with the count ("3 of 7"), and a "Done for today" state that lists what was recorded. `#/check/<id>`
   opens that card even when it is not due. A card with `reference: true` is a guide, not a record: it is never due, and the deck lists such cards under
   "Guides" below the stack. Export `cardsForTopic(topicId)` from
   `src/components/check/deck.js` for the Emergency screen's "Check this now" buttons. Add one example `cards.js` under `src/components/check/` to prove the
   frame; delete it before you finish.
8. Every interactive element in the kit is at least 44 by 44 px and has a visible `:focus-visible` ring. Text is
   12px or larger. Scrollbars are styled once, here, from the tokens.

## Done when

- The example card validates, saves, shows a result, offers Undo, and leaves the deck; back returns to it.
- A term opens and closes by mouse and keyboard.
- Nothing in `src/components/ui/` uses a literal colour or `!important`.
- Screenshots in `docs/evidence/T28/`: card empty, with an error, with a result, the toast, a term open, the
  steps list, tabs and chips, each in dark and light, plus the deck at 390.
