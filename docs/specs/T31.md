# T31: Body cards (the old Daily Checkup)

Batch 3. Daily Checkup stops being a page. Each body system is a card in the deck, built from `CheckCard`.

## You own

- `src/components/checkup/**` (rename to `src/components/body/**`, including `cards.js`)
- `src/scripts/checkup.js`, `checkup-flow.js`, `health-indicators.js`, their tests
- `public/assets/*.png` body images that only the old checkup used (delete if unused)

Read only: `src/scripts/astronaut3d/index.js` (`mount`, `unmount`, `focus`), `src/data/emergencies.js`
(`VITALS_REFERENCE`), `src/data/topics.js`.

## Do

1. One card per body system that has measurements: brain and sleep, heart, lungs, kidneys, temperature and
   glucose, bones, muscles. `cards.js` exports them with `topic` set.
2. Layout of a body card: inputs on the left, the 3D model on the right through the card's illustration slot
   (`mount` on show, `focus(part)`, `unmount` on leave). If mounting fails or WebGL is missing, the card shows no
   illustration and still works. On phone the illustration is hidden.
3. Each input has its unit and its accepted range under it, and a number box. No separate slider under the
   number box (owner correction). An out-of-range value shows the error under the field with the range, and
   takes focus.
4. The health-indicator gauges are the sliders (owner correction): each gauge can be dragged and clicked to set
   its value, and stays in step with its number box both ways; keyboard arrows move it too (`role="slider"` with
   `aria-valuemin`, `aria-valuemax`, `aria-valuenow`). Normal ranges for vital signs appear as numbers on the
   gauge itself (ends and current value labelled), using `VITALS_REFERENCE`. Before a value is entered the gauge
   has no marker and no colour.
5. After saving, the card shows the result and one action. When the result is `warn` or `urgent` and the topic
   has an emergency card, the action is a button that opens it. Save calls `toast` with Undo, which removes the
   record just written.
6. Remove: the guided "STEP n/m" pane, the History and What-to-do panes (Progress and Home hold them), the
   "Daily checkup" title on the stage, the mission badge that opened Settings, any way to fill values the
   astronaut did not enter.
7. The N-telopeptide range still follows sex and menopause from the profile.

## Must survive

These exist today and are not to be lost by this ticket.

- **Device instructions.** The pulse oximeter, blood pressure cuff, glucometer and thermometer steps in
  `src/data/devices.js` show in the card that takes that measurement, through `Steps`, collapsed by default.
- **Emergency vital-signs check.** Today the Emergency guide lets the astronaut type vital signs and see them
  judged at once. Export one component for that (all vital-sign inputs on one card, no deck) so T35 can open it
  at `#/check/vitals` from the Emergency index.
- **What was recorded.** After the last body card, the deck's done state lists the values saved today.

## Done when

- From Home, a due body check opens its card; saving it removes it from the deck and from Home's list.
- A heart rate of 500 is refused with the accepted range under the field.
- Undo removes the saved record and returns the card.
- With WebGL disabled the cards still save.
- Screenshots in `docs/evidence/T31/`: a card empty, with an error, with a result, with a warning and its
  emergency button, at 1440 and 390, dark and light.
