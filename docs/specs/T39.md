Problems you find inside a section's own components go in your report with file and line; do not fix them here.

## Do

1. **Phone, 390 by 800.** No element wider than its container on any route (check `scrollWidth` of every
   element, with the demo mission loaded). Bottom tab bar clear of the content and of the toast. Dialogs are full
   screen. Every target at least 44 by 44 px.
2. **Keyboard.** Tab order follows reading order on every route. Shortcuts, shown in a "Keyboard" list in the
   profile: `E` Emergency, `H` Home, `C` Check, `P` Progress, `/` focus search on Emergency, `Esc` closes a
   dialog or leaves a card. None fire while typing in a field.
3. **Structure.** One `h1` per route, no skipped heading levels, landmarks for the bar, main and footer, a "Skip
   to content" link, no interactive element nested in another, every icon-only button named, `aria-current` on
   the active destination.
4. **Status.** The toast region and form errors are announced (`role="status"`, `aria-describedby`,
   `aria-invalid`). Route changes move focus to the `h1`.
5. **Offline.** The offline indicator sits in the top bar as one small labelled icon, not a banner; the update
   notice uses `toast`.
6. **Zoom.** At 200% browser zoom at 1440 wide, no content is cut off.
