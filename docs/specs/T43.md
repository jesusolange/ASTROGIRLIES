# T43: One device, several crew members

Batch 4 (needs T29 merged). A crew shares one tablet. The cover becomes "Who is checking in?": pick your name,
enter your PIN, and see only your own records. Nothing is shared between crew members; each person exports their
own data or report for the flight surgeon. There is no crew or doctor view.

## You own

- `src/scripts/storage.js`, `src/scripts/people.js` (new), `test/astromed-storage.test.js`, `test/people.test.js`
- `src/scripts/sync.js` (only so sync reads and writes the active person's data)
- The cover: `src/scripts/cover.js` or the cover component T29 left, and `src/components/crew/**` (new, with
  `crew.css`; do not add rules to `src/styles/`, T38 owns it in this batch)
- `src/components/profile/**` (one "Lock" action and one "Remove this person" action only)
- `src/scripts/mission.js` (`hasCrew` only)

Do not edit `src/components/shell/**`, `ui/**`, `check/**` (T39 owns them in this batch).

## Do

1. **People.** `src/scripts/people.js` keeps the list of people on this device under one key outside any
   person's data: `{ id, name, pinSalt, pinHash }`. Pure functions with tests: add, remove, verify PIN, active
   person.
2. **Each person's data is separate.** `storage.js` stores every app key under the active person. Callers keep
   using `store.get("astromed-…")` and `store.set(…)` unchanged; the storage layer adds and strips the person
   prefix. `appKeys`, `exportAll` and `importAll` see only the active person's keys and keep today's file format,
   so an export from one person can be imported by that person on another device. Switching person notifies
   subscribers so every screen re-reads.
3. **Existing data.** On first run after this change, data already on the device becomes the first person (name
   from the profile, no PIN). Nothing is lost; cover this with a test.
4. **PIN.** 4 to 8 digits, optional. Store only a salted hash made with the browser's Web Crypto. On the screen
   where a PIN is set, say once: "The PIN keeps your records out of casual view on this device. It is not
   encryption, and it cannot be recovered if forgotten." Label that as our design choice. Do not store the PIN.
5. **Cover as sign-in.** Over the planet background: "Who is checking in?", one large button per person (initials
   and name), then the PIN field if that person has one, with the error under the field. "Add crew member" opens
   the first-run profile for a new person. The mission facts and "Watch first" line show after a person is
   chosen. With one person and no PIN the cover behaves as T29 left it.
6. **Emergency without sign-in.** The Emergency button on the cover opens the guide for anyone. In that state
   nothing is recorded and no personal data is shown; checks opened from an emergency card ask for sign-in first.
7. **Lock.** "Lock" in the profile returns to the cover. The app also locks after 10 minutes without input (our
   design choice, labelled in the profile) and when the tab has been hidden for that long.
8. **Remove a person.** In the profile, after typing the person's name to confirm, removes that person and their
   records. A person whose PIN is forgotten can be removed the same way from the cover; say plainly that their
   records are deleted and suggest nothing else.
9. **Crew wording.** `hasCrew()` is true when the profile's crew size is over 1 or when more than one person is
   on this device.
10. **Sync and demo data.** Sign-in to the optional sync and "Load demo mission" apply to the active person only.

## Must survive

- Export, import and CSV export work exactly as before for the active person.
- A device with one person and no PIN needs no extra tap compared with today.
- The app still works with no network; no account or server is needed for any of this.
- No screen, export or report shows one person's records to another.
