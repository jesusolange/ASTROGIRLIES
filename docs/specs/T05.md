# T05: Mental health results in place, with history and an urgent response

Ticket 12, and the "report" versus "protocols" naming from ticket 8, in `docs/tickets.md`.

## Why

After a mental health questionnaire the app redirects to Overview instead of showing the result. There is no
history. A non-zero answer to the suicidal-thoughts question gets no immediate response.

## You own

- `src/scripts/sections.js` except the navigation block (`PAGES`, `show`, `register`, `AstroMedNav` and the three
  click handlers after them), which is the coordinator's
- `src/scripts/mental.js` and `src/styles/mental.css` (new, if you split code out; `sections.js` is over 600 lines,
  so move the mental health code into `mental.js` and import it from `sections.js`)
- `test/mental.test.js` for the scoring helpers

## Do

- Show the result of each questionnaire inside the Mental Health section, under the form: the score, the band, what
  it means in one sentence, and "What to do" with concrete actions the astronaut can take alone or with the crew.
  No redirect to Overview.
- History tab or panel in the section: every saved result with date, questionnaire, score and band, newest first,
  and the change against the previous result of the same questionnaire. Store through
  `window.AstroMedStore.store`; keep reading results that are already stored under the existing keys.
- Suicidal-thoughts item (the PHQ-9 style item about thoughts of being better off dead or hurting yourself): when
  the answer is anything other than "Not at all", show an immediate, calm, prominent response as soon as the form
  is submitted, above the score: tell a crew member you trust now (name the role: crew medical officer or
  commander), do not stay alone, and a button "Open Emergencies" that calls `window.AstroMedNav.show("emergencies")`
  when that page exists. Do not write clinical advice beyond that. The final wording comes from the team's medic:
  put the text in one constant at the top of the module and list it under `SOURCE-NEEDED:` in your report.
- This response must not depend on the total score, and must be recorded in history as flagged.
- Keep `window.AstroMedData`, `analyze()` and `findings`-style output working: the PDF report
  (`public/legacy/astromed-pro.js`) and other modules read them. Make mental health results appear in `analyze()`
  with a `todo` action if they do not already.
- Naming: where the UI says "report" for what the sidebar calls "Protocols", say "protocols". The PDF itself stays
  "report".
- Do not remove the Overview page or its sidebar button: a later task replaces it with the Today page.

## Done when

- Completing a questionnaire shows the result and actions in the section, and it appears in History after a
  reload.
- Answering the suicidal-thoughts item with any option other than "Not at all" shows the immediate response,
  whatever the other answers are; answering "Not at all" does not.
- Unit tests cover the scoring bands and the flag.
- Screenshots in `docs/evidence/T05/`: a result, the history, the urgent response.
