# T24: No reassurance without a measurement

Batch 1. The score reads 100 "Ready" from one recorded area, the checkup can be filled with mean values, and the
stress questionnaire starts on "None". A green signal with no measurement behind it is the most dangerous output
of a self-monitoring aid.

## You own

- `src/scripts/readiness.js`, `today.js`, `missions.js`, `checkup.js`, `checkup-flow.js`
- `src/components/today/**`, `src/components/checkup/**` (except `CheckupPage.jsx`, which T21 creates)
- `src/components/mental/MentalHealthView.jsx`, `Questionnaire.jsx`
- Tests for those modules

## Do

1. **Coverage threshold.** One pure function gives the share of due areas that have a current record. Below 50%
   there is no number, no ring colour and no "Ready" word: the hero says "Not enough data: 2 of 7 areas recorded"
   and lists the missing checks as buttons. At 50% or more the score shows with "from n of m areas" in normal
   body-size text beside it. Label the 50% as our design choice.
2. Remove the rocket and every other emoji in your files. Remove levels, titles ("Cadet"), points and badges from
   the screen; keep only the streak count and the day's completion (done / due). No reward animation.
3. Remove the "fill with mean values" action and any other way to fill the checkup with values the astronaut did
   not enter. Inputs start empty; a gauge with no value is neutral, not green.
4. Sliders in the checkup can be dragged and typed; the number and the slider stay in step.
5. Stress questionnaire answers start unset, like the depression form. "See my result" is blocked until every
   question is answered, with the error under the first unanswered question and focus on it.
6. Home text: remove the repeated sentence "An area with no recent data counts as missing, not as healthy." (say
   it once, in the score explanation) and "that share is our design choice" where it repeats. "What is missing"
   and "Due today" become one list.
7. Remove the "Training and devices" panel from Home. Keep its device instructions as data for T34.
8. Text in your files follows T23's rules (no other person in the first step; no emoji; no developer notes). Do
   not import `mission.js`; where a line needs the flight surgeon, leave it as a second sentence for T27.

## Done when

- A new profile with one saved check shows no score and lists the missing checks.
- With four of seven areas recorded the score shows, with its coverage beside it.
- The checkup opens empty; nothing fills it but the astronaut.
- An untouched stress form cannot be submitted.
- Tests cover the coverage function at 0, just under and at 50%.
- Screenshots in `docs/evidence/T24/`: Home under and over the threshold, the empty checkup, the blocked stress
  form, in dark and light.
