# T30: Repair the 3D astronaut

Batch 2, or earlier: this ticket depends on nothing and nothing waits for it. The model is an illustration beside
the body cards. If it is not right by the deadline it is hidden; the cards work without it.

## You own

- `src/scripts/astronaut3d/**`, `src/styles/astronaut3d.css`, `public/assets/models/**`
- `test/**` for logic you add

Do not edit the checkup components; expose what they need from `src/scripts/astronaut3d/index.js`.

## Do

1. Remove the outline of head and body built from primitives.
2. One brain, inside the head, correct size and orientation. Remove the large semi-transparent one and the small
   displaced one.
3. Lungs sit in the upper chest, either side of the midline, not translated out of the body. Heart sits between
   the lungs, slightly left of the midline. Kidneys sit at the back of the upper abdomen, either side of the
   spine. Check each from front, side and back.
4. Glow: at most one accent on the selected part. Unselected parts have no glow.
5. Camera: selecting a part frames it; selecting temperature frames the whole body; selecting eyes zooms on the
   head.
6. Remove from the stage: the "Daily checkup" text, the profile button, "STEP n/m", and any credit text (credits
   are in Sources).
7. Public interface from `index.js`: `mount(element)`, `unmount()`, `focus(part)` with parts `brain`, `heart`,
   `lungs`, `kidneys`, `bones`, `muscles`, `body`, `eyes`. No DOM lookups outside the element given to `mount`.
8. Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`: camera moves cut instead of animating.

## Done when

- Each of the eight parts, selected in turn, is in the anatomically expected place and framed.
- Nothing glows except the selected part.
- Screenshots in `docs/evidence/T30/`: front, side and back with the suit open; each part focused; light theme.
