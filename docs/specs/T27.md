# T27: Home, and the one table of topics

Batch 2. Today becomes Home. One table ties each topic to its check, its NASA risks and its emergency card, so
every cross-link is generated.

## You own

- `src/data/topics.js` (new), `src/data/risk-coverage.js` (delete), `test/topics.test.js` (new)
- `src/scripts/today.js` (rename to `home.js`), `readiness.js`, `missions.js`, their tests
- `src/components/today/**` (rename to `src/components/home/**`)

Read only: `src/scripts/risks.js`, `src/data/hrr-risks.js`, `src/data/emergencies.js`, `src/scripts/mission.js`.

## Do

1. **`src/data/topics.js`.** One row per topic: `{ id, label, riskIds, cards, emergency }`. Start from
   `risk-coverage.js` (bones, kidneys, immune, radiation, mental, sleep, vision, nutrition, heart, exercise). The
   mapping is our design choice and is labelled so where it is shown. `emergency` is the id of the matching card
   in `src/data/emergencies.js` or `null` (vision: `vision-change`; immune: `fever`; radiation: `storm`;
   heart: `breath`). Leave `cards` out of the rows: cards name their topic themselves and the deck groups them. Export
   `topicFor(riskId)`, `emergencyFor(topicId)`, `topicsFor(emergencyId)`.
2. **Home, top to bottom:**
   - Streak (days in a row with every due check done) and today's completion ring (done / due). Static.
   - Score, under the T24 coverage rule. "How this score is made" opens a dialog: the areas, their weights, the
     sentence that weights and intervals follow NASA's ratings for the chosen mission, and "Why this order" with
     the ranked risks (the content of the old Mission risks page, each with its NASA source link and "Not
     monitored yet" where no topic covers it).
   - Needs attention: urgent and warning findings. Each one whose topic has an emergency card has a button that
     calls `openEmergency(id)`.
   - Due today: one list, ordered by risk rating, each row opening its check with `openCard` (until batch 3, the
     section route T26 provides).
3. Intervals: keep `intervalFor(score)`; move it and `isDue`, `daysSince` out of `missions.js` into `home.js`.
   Delete the rest of `missions.js` (tasks, points, levels, badges, reminders) and `readiness.js` code that only
   served the old Readiness page.
4. Where T24 left a flight-surgeon sentence, build it with `alsoTell()`.
5. Remove the avatar "?" placeholder from Home; the profile button in the bar is the only profile entry.
