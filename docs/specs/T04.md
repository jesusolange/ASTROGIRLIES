# T04: One theme for the whole app

Ticket 9 in `docs/tickets.md`.

## Why

The checkup panel and the Settings dialog are light cards on a dark page; dropdowns and the email field use browser
defaults. The CSS is a light stylesheet (`style.css`), section styles (`sections.css`) and a dark override layer
(`dark.css`) full of `html body ...` selectors.

## You own

- `src/styles/style.css`, `src/styles/sections.css`, `src/styles/dark.css`, `src/styles/theme.css` (new, if useful)
- `src/scripts/theme.js` (already imported by `main.js`)
- In `src/pages/index.astro`: the `<head>` (colour-scheme meta, CSS imports in the frontmatter) and one new header
  bar at the top of `<main class="content">`
- `src/styles/tokens.css` is the coordinator's contract: you may add tokens and adjust values, but do not rename or
  remove existing `--am-*` names (other workers are using them right now)

Other workers are editing the section modules in `src/scripts/` at the same time. Several of those modules carry
their own CSS inside JavaScript strings. Do not edit those modules; list the literal colours you found in them in
your report so a follow-up can move them to tokens.

## Do

- Dark is the default. Every surface, text, border and state colour in the three stylesheets comes from the
  `--am-*` tokens. Remove the old `--bg, --surface, --ink ...` and `--d-*` variables by mapping them to tokens
  (keep an alias only where a JS module's inline CSS still uses the old name; say which).
- Fold `dark.css` into the base styles so that there is one theme layer, not a light sheet plus overrides.
- Style every form control: text and number inputs, `select` and its dropdown list (`color-scheme` on `:root` so
  the native list matches), textarea, checkbox, radio, range, the email field in the account section, buttons,
  focus rings (`--am-focus`), disabled state.
- Light and dark switch in the header: sets `data-theme="light"` or `"dark"` on `<html>`, remembers the choice with
  `window.AstroMedStore.store` under `astromed-theme`, applies it before first paint as far as possible, and
  follows `prefers-color-scheme` only when nothing is stored (default dark otherwise).
- Profile icon at the top right of the header: shows whether the user is signed in and the sync state
  (`window.AstroMedSync.session()` and its status; read `src/scripts/sync.js` for what it exposes), opens the
  account section of Settings on click. Signed-out is the normal state and must not look like an error.
- Contrast: body text at least 4.5:1 and large text and UI boundaries at least 3:1 against their background, in
  both themes. Write a small script under `tools/` or a test that computes the contrast of the token pairs you use
  for text, and include its output in your report.
- The 3D stage keeps its dark space background in both themes; the panels around it follow the theme.

## Done when

- No screen mixes light and dark surfaces, in either theme, and the switch changes every section.
- Screenshots in `docs/evidence/T04/`, dark and light, for: Daily Checkup, Settings dialog, profile dialog,
  Environment, Nutrition, Mental Health, Radiation, Missions, Progress, Protocols. Use
  `tools/shot.mjs --eval` to open each one. Open every image and look for a light card on dark or the reverse.
- The contrast output lists every text token pair with its ratio, all passing.
