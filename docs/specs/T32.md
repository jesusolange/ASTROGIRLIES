# T32: Vision as cards

Batch 3. The Vision page stacks calibration, grid, near chart and symptoms on one scroll. It becomes a short run
of cards.

## You own

- `src/components/vision/**` (including `cards.js`, `vision.css`), `src/scripts/vision.js`, `test/vision.test.js`

## Do

1. Cards, in order, all with `topic: "vision"`: calibrate the screen (once per device; skipped when stored),
   grid test left eye, grid test right eye, near chart left eye, near chart right eye, symptoms. The eye is fixed
   by the card; where a chooser remains, "Left eye" is on the left and "Right eye" on the right.
2. Instructions use `Steps` from the kit. This removes the doubled numbering ("1. 1.").
3. Near-chart rows that cannot be drawn at the calibrated size on this screen are not offered; say which rows
   were tested.
4. Grid cells: the grid stays as designed (it is the test), but the whole grid is one focusable control with
   arrow-key movement and Space to mark, and each cell's hit area is at least 24 px at the calibrated size.
5. The result (after the last card) follows the existing thresholds, labelled as our design choice once. A `warn`
   or `urgent` result shows a button that opens the emergency card for the topic (`emergencyFor("vision")`).
   Remove the "pending review by the team's medic" line and the separate To-do panel.
6. Remove "Latest saved results" and History from here; Progress shows them (T36 reads the same stored records;
   do not change their shape).
7. Selected chips meet 4.5:1 contrast in both themes (the selected "Right eye" chip is 3.32:1 in dark today).
8. The eye figure with its credit shows on the first card; the credit links to Sources.
9. Add a `:focus-visible` ring to every control in `vision.css`; remove the three side-stripe borders the
   detector flags at `vision.css` lines 129, 145, 160.

## Done when

- A result that needs attention offers the "Sudden change in vision" card.
- Keyboard only: the grid can be marked and saved.
